🧭What I think AI is for

If a task asks you to act like a robot, let a bot do it.

I don't think AI is here to replace people. Every job has tasks that nobody enjoys and everybody does, because there was never another option. Filing insurance claims. Untangling calendar clashes. Sorting notes into the right place. Those tasks ask us to be as mechanical as possible.

When a bot takes them, the time goes back to the human parts of work: the conversation, the judgement, the relationship.

Medical insurance claims were one of my most annoying jobs. Here is the same job, then and now.

  2. 🙋 MeSearch my email for them
  3. 🙋 MeDownload each invoice
  4. 🙋 MeRename each file, because the name is too long for the insurance app
  5. 🙋 MeUpload each one to the app
  6. 🙋 MeTry to remember whether I already filed it
NOW · 3 FOR ME, 3 FOR THE BOT
  1. 🤖 BotChecks my email for invoices every evening
  2. 🤖 BotSends them to my phone, already renamed for the app
  3. 🙋 MeDownload them from the chat
  4. 🙋 MeUpload them to the app
  5. 🙋 MeReply “done”
  6. 🤖 BotLogs the claim, so I can always see what I have filed

The upload is the only real step left for me, because the app needs a person to do it. In my own words: “I never check for invoices anymore.”

🤔Why a chat app is not enough

Most of us ask ChatGPT or Claude for help now, and the answers come fast. But a chat app only answers. The copying, checking and following up stay with you. The claim above only got lighter when something took over the whole job.

77%

This is common. Upwork surveyed 2,500 workers in 2024, and more than three in four of the employees who used AI said it had given them more work in at least one way. Source: Upwork Research Institute, “From Burnout to Balance”, July 2024.

What changed my days was not a better chat. It was an assistant that does whole jobs, the same way every time, and only asks me when a decision is mine.

🌱What opens up when the admin is handled

The hours matter. But the bigger change for me was what I did with the space.

Time for people

You have time to talk to your colleagues and the people you work with, instead of only messaging them about tasks.

Room to think bigger

You have the mental space to plan the personal and work projects you keep putting off.

You think like a startup

You know you can grow, but you hand jobs over instead of doing every one yourself.

Tools that fit you

Your assistant is shaped around how you work, and it changes when your needs change.

🔑Why build it yourself

You could wait for an app that does some of this. I think building your own is better, for three reasons.

  • It fits how you think. An app makes you work its way. Your assistant works your way, because you wrote its rules.
  • You can fix it. When something goes wrong, you know where to look. Nobody has to come and rescue you.
  • It is yours. Your files and rules live on your own server, and you choose what the AI can see.

🗓️How the course works

Six sessions of about three hours, one a week. Between sessions you try what you learned on your own work.

  1. 1

    What works in Hong Kong, and keeping your data private

    Which tools work here, which need a workaround, and whose laws reach your data. Please don't buy any AI subscription or server before this session.

  2. 2

    A system that fits how you think

    I show you my own system live. Then you sketch yours: your dashboard, your morning message, and where your notes live.

  3. 3

    Your work on paper

    You list the tasks you repeat every week, and the ones you hate. Then you introduce yourself to the AI: how you work and how you like things written.

  4. 4

    Rules, and your first build

    You write the rules your assistant must follow, then build its first real job from start to finish.

  5. 5

    Your assistant on your phone

    You set up your own private server and a chat bot you can message from your phone. I do the fiddly technical parts with you.

  6. 6

    Keeping it working

    What breaks and how to spot it early, how to add new jobs, and your plan for the first 30 days on your own.
